How the recommender works

It is a hybrid, content-based system. In My own mode only the items you select or add are used, and they are saved in this browser. Hard rules first remove items that do not suit the chosen occasion, weather and gender. Every valid combination is then generated: a dress or a top plus bottom, shoes, and an optional layer. Rain and cold weather always add a suitable layer. Each look gets a 0 to 100 score:

  • Occasion (30%): average formality against the occasion's target, minus a penalty when pieces clash in formality.
  • Weather (25%): average warmth against the target for the temperature.
  • Colour (20%): HSL colour harmony. Neutrals pair with anything, analogous and complementary hues score high, and too many bright colours lose points.
  • Vibe (15%): how many pieces match the vibes you picked.
  • Your taste (10%): per-item affinity learned from Like and Not for me taps.

The top three looks are picked greedily so they never repeat a main piece, and a dress look is included when one scores close to the best. Accessories are then chosen for each look by the same occasion, colour and vibe logic. Rainy looks always include an umbrella.
